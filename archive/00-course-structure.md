# Trading Course Structure (from intro videos)

Source: @anjanpoonacha / Champions Club

## Four Components of the Trading System

### 1. Trading Setup
- Set of rules/guidelines that dictate trading
- Defines **what** and **when** to buy and sell
- Based on technical analysis
- Must be logical and have an **edge** (positive expectancy)
- Not just mixing indicators — needs sound logic behind it

### 2. Risk Management
- Stop losses
- Position sizing
- Risk-reward ratios
- Win rate
- Intensity metrics
- Expectancy metrics
- Capital allocation

### 3. Trading Psychology
- Act with logic, not emotions
- Discipline
- Daily mindset affirmations (read every morning)

### 4. Daily Routine
- When to analyze
- When to trade
- Screen time management
- Journaling (maintain records of every trade)
- Trading diary updates
