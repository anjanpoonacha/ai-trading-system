# Stage Analysis Reference

## Four Stock Stages

| Stage | Name | Price Behavior | Duration | Key Activity |
|-------|------|----------------|----------|--------------|
| 1 | Accumulation | Sideways/range-bound | Months to years | Big players slowly buying |
| 2 | Growth | Higher highs + higher lows | 1-3+ years | Continuous demand, uptrend |
| 3 | Distribution | Sideways/range-bound | Variable | Big players slowly selling |
| 4 | Decline | Lower lows + lower highs | Variable | Continuous supply, downtrend |

---

## Stage 1: Accumulation

### Characteristics
- Trendless sideways price action for months/years after decline
- No sustainable demand or supply
- Rounding bottom patterns common
- Volume spikes indicate institutional buying

### Big Player Activity
- **Early Phase**: Slow, gradual buying
- **Late Phase**: Aggressive buying triggers breakout → Stage 2
- Breakout occurs BEFORE public news/announcements

### Accumulation vs. Base (Within Stage 2)
- **Accumulation**: After decline, extended sideways (multi-month/year), precedes Stage 2
- **Base**: During uptrend, shallow consolidation, part of ongoing Stage 2
- **Exception**: Very deep pullback in Stage 2 may be reclassified as accumulation

---

## Stage 2: Growth (Most Important)

### Core Requirements
- **Trend Structure**: Higher highs AND higher lows
- **Price vs 200 DMA**: Above 200 DMA throughout
- **200 DMA Slope**: Trending up ≥1 month
- **50 DMA Position**: Above 200 DMA throughout
- **Volume Transition**: Clear increase from decline/accumulation phase
- **Demand > Supply**: Bulls dominant, buyers pay progressively higher prices

### Stage 2 Features

| Feature | Requirement | Notes |
|---------|------------|-------|
| Price vs 50 DMA | Sustains above 50 DMA | In very strong Stage 2 |
| Bases & Breakouts | Frequent | Stock consolidates, then breaks out |
| Pivotal Candles | Frequent positive pivotal candles | Big green candles with volume |
| Volume Pattern | Up swings: high volume<br>Down swings: low volume | Healthy characteristic |
| Sectoral Strength | Similar-sector stocks strong | Stocks move in groups |
| Fundamental Confirmation | Positive results, rising sales/profits | Appears after price advance |

### Base Patterns in Stage 2

| Base Type | Characteristics | Significance |
|-----------|----------------|--------------|
| Small bases | Shallow consolidations | Healthy Stage 2 - stock keeps advancing |
| Large/deep bases | Extended consolidation with significant pullback | May indicate accumulation within Stage 2 |
| Mini base breakout | Minor resistance broken before major breakout | Often precedes strong Stage 2 move |

### Volume Analysis
- **At breakout**: Large volume expansion
- **During uptrend**: Volumes keep rising
- **Green bars (up moves)**: High volume
- **Red bars (pullbacks)**: Low/average volume
- **Within accumulation**: Institutional volume spikes = serious demand

### Stage 2 Transition Patterns
**Clear Accumulation → Stage 2**
- Defined sideways structure (Stage 1)
- Breakout from accumulation resistance
- Transition to uptrend

**Direct Transition (No Clear Stage 1)**
- Stock moves from Stage 4 directly to Stage 2
- Immediate higher highs and higher lows
- No defined sideways accumulation

### Duration
- Healthy growth stages: 1+ years
- Can extend to 2-3 years

---

## Stage 3: Distribution

### Trigger Conditions
Big players determine stock is:
- Overvalued (price exceeds intrinsic value)
- Unable to grow further
- Facing potential business deterioration

### Characteristics
- Sideways consolidation after advance
- **Buyers**: Retail investors (uninformed)
- **Sellers**: Big players (informed)
- Demand insufficient to maintain uptrend
- Rounding top patterns common

### Breakdown Mechanics
- **Late Phase**: Big players sell aggressively
- Breakdown below distribution range with negative pivotal candle + volume
- Transition to Stage 4

---

## Stage 4: Decline

### Characteristics
- **Trend Structure**: Lower lows AND lower highs
- **Price vs 200 DMA**: Price stays below 200 DMA
- **200 DMA Slope**: Slopes downward for extended period
- **50 DMA Position**: Trades below 200 DMA and sustains there
- **Support Breaks**: Supports broken repeatedly
- **Pivotal Candles**: Both positive AND negative pivotal candles (unlike Stage 2)
- **Volume Pattern**: Big red volume bars on down moves

### Key Insight: Charts Lead Fundamentals
- Company may still report good earnings while in Stage 4
- Bad news/earnings appear only after significant decline
- When fundamentals contradict technicals → **trust the price**

### Transition Out of Stage 4
When big players sense undervaluation/turnaround:
- Start buying slowly → new Stage 1 (Accumulation) → Breakout → Stage 2

---

## Breakout Analysis

### Valid Breakout Characteristics
- **Volume**: Above-average volume on breakout candle
- **Candle**: Positive pivotal candle (large green candle)
- **Follow-through**: Price continues higher after breakout
- **Support**: Previous resistance becomes support

### Failed/Fake Breakout
- Breakout followed by immediate reversal
- Low volume on breakout
- No follow-through buying
- Price falls back below breakout level

### Resistance Breakout Levels
- **Top of accumulation**: Major breakout confirming Stage 2
- **Mini base resistance**: Minor breakout within larger structure
- **Prior highs**: Breaking previous peaks extends Stage 2

---

## Critical Trading Rules

### When to Buy
**ONLY in Stage 2 (Growth)**
- Never buy in Stage 4 (Decline)
- Stock in decline has higher probability to continue down

### When to Sell
- Stage 2 violated (breakdown below 200 DMA with negative pivotal candle)
- Distribution confirmed
- Stock transitions to Stage 4

### Red Flags in Stage 2
1. Large negative pivotal candle with big volume
2. Price breaks below 200 DMA
3. Support levels broken
4. Change in structure (lower highs, lower lows)
5. 50 DMA crosses below 200 DMA
6. Wide range candle differing from normal Stage 2 behavior
7. Trend intensity decreased significantly
8. Stock at same level for many months
9. Very large bases without continuation

### NOT Stage 2 - Key Disqualifiers
- Sideways/range-bound action for extended period (months/years)
- Major support level broken
- Price below 200-day MA
- Moving averages flattening or declining
- Slowed momentum/topping patterns

---

## Volume Analysis Rules

### Volume Transition
**Decline to Advance**: Clear increase in volume when transitioning from Stage 4 to Stage 2
- **Decline**: Low, subdued volume
- **Growth**: Significantly higher volume bars

### Volume on Breakdowns
- **Low Volume Breakdown**: Less reliable, may not continue
- **High Volume Breakdown**: Confirms distribution, likely to continue

### Volume on Corrections
- **Within Stage 2**: Pullbacks should occur on lower volume than advances
- **Lack of negative pivotal candles**: No large red candles with high volume during corrections

## Index Analysis

### Key Indexes by Market Cap
- **Nifty 50**: Large cap representation
- **Nifty 500**: Broad market (preferred for analysis)
- **Nifty Midcap 100**
- **Nifty Smallcap 100**
- **Sector Indexes**: CNX Auto, Pharma, PSE, Realty, etc.

### Market Phase Identification

| Index Stage | Market Condition | Stock Behavior |
|-------------|------------------|----------------|
| Nifty 500 in Stage 2 | Bull Market | Many stocks in Stage 2, few in Stage 4 |
| Nifty 500 in Stage 4 | Bear Market | Many stocks in Stage 4, few in Stage 2 |

**Principle**: Stocks move in groups
- Bull market: Many stocks advancing together
- Bear market: Many stocks declining together

**Analysis Preference**: Use Nifty 500 over Nifty 50 for broader market representation (500 stocks vs. 50)

## Critical Concepts

### Price Movement Requirements
- **Uptrend (Stage 2)**: Requires continuous, sustainable demand
- **Downtrend (Stage 4)**: Requires continuous, excessive supply
- **Sideways (Stages 1 & 3)**: No sustainable demand OR supply

### Information Asymmetry
- Big players act on information before it becomes public
- Chart patterns reveal this activity
- Breakouts/breakdowns precede news announcements

### Big Player Constraints
- Cannot enter/exit positions quickly
- Require extended timeframes (days/weeks/months) to build/unwind positions
- This constraint creates observable accumulation/distribution patterns

### Change in Trend Confirmation
Wait for confirmation before declaring new Stage 2:
- Resistance must be broken with big volume
- Moving averages must slope up

### Flexibility Required
- Not all Stage 2 moves show every feature
- Understand overall structure and context
- Sometimes Stage 1 not clearly visible
- Focus on identifying clean Stage 2 vs. everything else

## Practical Classification Process

### Daily Homework Protocol
1. Analyze 10 stocks per day on weekly chart
2. Review entire history from listing date
3. Identify all stage transitions
4. Note features of each stage
5. Check daily chart for confirmation

**Timeline**: 
- 1 month = 300 stocks analyzed
- 3 months = 900 stocks analyzed
- 6 months = mastery level

### Stage Transition Sequence
**Typical Pattern**: Stage 4 (Decline) → Stage 1 (Accumulation) → Stage 2 (Advance) → Stage 3 (Distribution) → Stage 4

**Variations**:
- Direct Stage 4 → Stage 2 (no clear accumulation)
- Extended Stage 1 (multi-year accumulation)
- Brief Stage 3 (quick distribution)
