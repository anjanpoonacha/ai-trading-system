# Champion Swing Short (CSS) Reference

## Overview
- **Strategy Type**: Short selling (sell first, buy back later at lower price)
- **Time Horizon**: 5–20 days
- **Analysis Timeframes**: Weekly + Daily
- **Trading Timeframe**: Daily (entries/exits)
- **Time Required**: 20 minutes/day (with practice)
- **Market Segment**: Futures only (~200 stocks in India)

---

## Position in Trading Approach
- **Primary approach**: Long-side trading
- **CSS is OPTIONAL** — not mandatory for profitability
- Use CSS to balance trading in weak market conditions
- Do NOT become primarily a short-side trader
- Goal: Maximize profits, not reduce long-side gains
- Intensity is discretionary — market conditions dictate number of positions

---

## Futures Trading Requirements (India)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Minimum contract value | ~₹7 lakhs |
| Average stop-loss | 3–4% |
| Minimum risk per trade | ₹20,000–30,000 |
| Trading unit | Lots (not quantities) |
| Example: Reliance lot size | 250 shares |

**Requirement**: Only trade CSS if capital and risk appetite allow for ₹20k–30k risk per trade.

---

## Entry Conditions

### Index Chart (Nifty 500 for India)
1. **10 and 20 DMA must be sloping down**
2. **Pullback toward 10 or 20 DMA** — wait for index to pull back UP after a down move
3. Do NOT enter during:
   - Initial breakdown (wait for pullback)
   - Extended downswings
   - Clear change in character (PPC + gap up above 20 DMA)

### Stock Chart
1. **Recent distribution characteristics**:
   - Head & Shoulders
   - Rounding tops
   - Frequent NPCs
   - Breakdown of stage 2/3 structure
2. **Pullback toward 20, 50, or 200 DMA** (prefer 50/200)
3. **Confirmation candle**:
   - Volume expansion (1.3–1.6x) OR
   - TRP expansion
   - Small consolidation (3–6 bars) before breakdown preferred
4. **Entry trigger**: Break of previous day's low

### Avoid Extended Entries
- Do NOT enter if confirmation candle is substantially away from previous swing high
- Prefer entries immediately after:
  - Upswing + consolidation
  - Clean pullback structure
- Skip if 4–5 consecutive red candles already occurred

---

## Stop Loss
- **Formula**: 1.5 × Average TRP (broader than CDS due to stage 3/4 volatility)
- **Handling**: Same as CDS — allow 30–90 min sustain beyond stop before exiting
- **Placement options**:
  - TRP-based level
  - Technical level (swing high, resistance)
  - 50/50 combination

---

## Exit Rules

### General Principles
1. **Exit only on close** (last 30 min) — except stop-loss
2. **Exit MORE QUICKLY than CDS** — settle for lower R multiples
3. **Holding period**: Typically 7 days or less
4. **Pullbacks are SHARP** when stocks fall — use tight trailing stops

### Exit Triggers
| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| 1–1.5R at support | Exit 30% |
| 2R or large NPC | Exit 50% |
| Extended downswing (4–5 bars) | Exit 30–50% |
| Index extended downside | Book partial/full gains |
| Gap down after profit | Exit remaining position |
| Trailing stop hit | Exit remaining |

### Index-Based Exits
- **Use index chart** to time exits (unlike long trades)
- When index shows extended downswing (4–5 consecutive down bars, 4–5% drop):
  - Book partial profits (30–50%)
  - Tighten trailing stops

### Trailing Stops
- After first exit, trail stop to recent swing high (last 3–4 candles)
- Adjust tighter as stock moves in your favor
- Exit if sharp pullback breaks trailing stop

---

## Earnings Management
- **Do NOT take trades if earnings in next 1–3 days**
- If holding without profit cushion before earnings: **EXIT**
- If holding with profit cushion: May hold through earnings (discretionary)

---

## Pattern Recognition

### Distribution Structures
- Rounding tops
- Head & Shoulders
- Breakout failures (upswing → immediate reversal)
- Multiple NPCs in topping zone
- Breakdown of consolidation after stage 2

### Stage Transitions
- **Stage 3**: Distribution phase (topping structure, sideways)
- **Stage 3B**: Breakdown confirmed, possible stage 4 entry
- **Stage 4**: Clear downtrend, 10/20 DMA sloping down, below 200 DMA

---

## Example Trade Flow

1. **Index**: 10/20 DMA down, pullback to 10 or 20 DMA
2. **Stock**: Distribution pattern, pullback to 50/200 DMA, 3–6 bar consolidation
3. **Entry**: Break of previous day's low with volume/TRP expansion
4. **Stop**: 1.5 × TRP or technical level
5. **Exit 1** (30%): 1–1.5R or at support
6. **Exit 2** (40–50%): 2R or large NPC
7. **Exit 3** (remaining): Trailing stop hit or index extended

---

## Key Differences from CDS

| Aspect | CDS | CSS |
|--------|-----|-----|
| Stop-loss | 1.0 × TRP | 1.5 × TRP |
| Exit speed | Moderate | Quick (7 days typical) |
| R multiple target | 2–3R+ | 1.5–2R acceptable |
| Index use | Entry only | Entry + Exit timing |
| Pullback character | Smooth | Sharp (exit quickly) |
| Number of exits | 2–3 | Often 2 |

---

## Risk Management
- **Capital requirement**: Sufficient for ₹20k–30k risk per trade
- **Position sizing**: Based on 1.5 × TRP stop-loss
- **Portfolio balance**: Discretionary mix of longs/shorts based on market conditions
- **Avoid**: Shorting in super bullish markets (focus 100% on longs)

---

## Market Condition Guidelines

| Market State | Action |
|--------------|--------|
| Super bullish | No shorts, 100% long focus |
| Moderately bullish | Conservative shorts (if any) |
| Choppy/weak | Balanced long/short approach |
| Clearly bearish | Aggressive shorts, very conservative longs |
| Change in character (PPC + gap up) | Stop new shorts, reassess |

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
1. Taking extended entries (4–5 red candles already)
2. Shorting on first pullback without consolidation
3. Holding through sharp pullbacks (exit quickly)
4. Ignoring index extended downswings for exits
5. Trading CSS without sufficient capital for ₹20k–30k risk
6. Becoming primarily a short-side trader
